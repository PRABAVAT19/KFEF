Who We Are

Working so children can learn well-fed

KFEF is a community-based organisation founded in Moroto in 2014 to fight hunger in schools and improve children’s learning.

Our team: a director, a program manager, and five field volunteers.

Our Mission

To eliminate hunger in schools and improve academic performance.

Our Vision

A Karamoja where no child learns on an empty stomach.

Our Objectives Provide school meals Sensitize parents Support farmers

Our Programs

1

School Feeding

Providing porridge and lunch in five schools.

2

Parent Sensitization

Training parents on the importance of breakfast.

3

School Gardens

Helping schools start gardens to grow vegetables.

4

Performance Monitoring

Tracking attendance and marks before and after feeding.
